About the book

A step-by-step guide to using ChatGPT and prompt engineering to create and sell content and services. It is written for beginners and advanced users alike, with no coding required.

  • An introduction to AI, ChatGPT and the basics of prompt engineering.
  • Strategies and techniques for writing effective prompts, and an iterative approach to testing and improving them.
  • Practical income ideas: podcasts, city and travel guides, cookbooks, fiction and non-fiction books, affiliate marketing, online courses, social media management, video scripts, calendars, self-help materials, games, puzzles and quizzes.
  • Ready-to-use example prompts in every chapter.
